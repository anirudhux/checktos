**Introduction.** "These Terms of Service (\"Terms\") and our Platform Rules constitute a legal agreement between you and Lovable Labs Incorporated... to use lovable.dev (the \"Site\") and other products and services... (the \"Services\")." "For material changes, we will provide at least thirty (30) days' advance notice by email or through the Services before they take effect... your continued use of the Services after the changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised Terms."

**Definitions.** Defines "AI Gateway", "Credits" ("prepaid, non-refundable, non-redeemable units"), "Customer Data", "Infrastructure Provider(s)" (e.g., Supabase), "Lovable Cloud", "Lovable Materials", "Platform", "Third-Party AI Provider(s)" (e.g., OpenAI, Google, or Anthropic), "Usage Data".

**Your Account.** Minimum age 18. Account creation and accuracy. Organization Accounts: "Managed account administrators may access, manage, retain, suspend, or terminate the account and its contents, including any associated Customer Data."

**License to Use Our Services.** "we grant you a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, worldwide, revocable license to access and use the Services... This license terminates immediately if you violate these Terms or your account is terminated for any reason."

**License Restrictions.** A list including no copying/reselling; no reverse engineering; no automated access without permission; "use the Services, AI Output, or prompts to train, develop, or improve competing AI models"; "use AI Output without appropriate review in high-risk or sensitive contexts (including medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical uses)."

**Subdomain Usage and Management.** "Lovable retains all ownership rights in the lovable.app domain and all subdomains. We reserve the right, at our sole discretion, to reclaim, reassign, redirect, suspend, or terminate any subdomain at any time and for any reason... We will generally provide a 7 days' advance notice before reclaiming a subdomain... You have no proprietary, possessory, or ongoing rights to any specific subdomain." Usernames: similar reclamation right.

**Subscriptions, Fees, and Payments.** "Except where required by law, subscription fees are non-refundable." Credits: prepaid, non-refundable, non-redeemable; frozen on cancellation until expiry. Purchasing and Auto-Reload via Stripe. Invoices: "our usage metering, billing records, and our payment processor's records, calculations, and decisions... are authoritative and, absent an obvious error, final and binding." Taxes.

**Term, Suspension, and Termination.** Your right to terminate anytime. "We may terminate your account or access to the Services for convenience by giving you advance notice... We may also suspend or terminate your account... immediately, without prior notice, if we reasonably believe you have materially breached these Terms." Credit Forfeiture on breach/fraud/abuse termination. "Except where required by applicable law, all fees are non-refundable."

**Service Dependencies and Availability.** No guarantee of availability; not responsible for third-party providers or force majeure.

**Beta or Experimental Features.** "provided \"as is\" and \"as available,\" without warranties... may be modified, suspended, or discontinued without notice and without liability to you."

**AI Output.** "AI Output may contain errors... You are solely responsible for reviewing, validating, and using any AI Output... Credits are consumed by each AI action based on the effort and resources used, regardless of the outcome, and are non-refundable and non-restorable even where the AI Output is erroneous."

**Your Content and Data.**
- Rights in Customer Data: "You grant us a worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free license to use, copy, modify, process, analyze, and otherwise exploit your Customer Data for our business purposes, including without limitation: operating, maintaining, and improving the Services; developing and training artificial intelligence and machine learning models; creating benchmarks, analytics, and insights; and any other lawful business purpose." "You may tell us at any time that you do not want your Customer Data used for model training or the other business purposes... and we will honor that request for prospective use, free of charge and regardless of your plan." De-identified data retained perpetually. "our Privacy Policy... governs and prevails over this section to the extent of any conflict."
- No Sensitive Data: "you agree not to upload... any protected health information subject to HIPAA, or other special or sensitive categories of data..."
- Your Responsibilities: you are responsible for rights, consents, and lawful basis.
- Use of Infrastructure and AI Providers: inputs transmitted to AI systems; "we do not guarantee that data transmitted through the Platform or the AI Gateway will be processed or stored in any particular country or region."

**Ownership.** Our Rights: Lovable owns Services and Lovable Materials and all Usage Data. Your Rights: "you own your Customer Data... you also own any AI Output generated for you... subject to any third-party rights in the underlying models, training data, or outputs."

**Disclaimers of Warranties.** "THE SERVICES... ARE PROVIDED \"AS IS\" AND \"AS AVAILABLE.\"... WE DISCLAIM ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES..."

**Indemnification.** "You agree to indemnify and hold us harmless from any claims, damages, losses, or expenses (including reasonable legal fees)" for misuse, non-compliance, third-party provider term violations, and your content.

**Limitation of Liability.** "IN NO EVENT WILL OUR TOTAL LIABILITY FOR ALL CLAIMS... EXCEED THE AMOUNT YOU PAID US FOR THE SERVICES IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE CLAIM, EXCEPT TO THE EXTENT SUCH LIABILITY CANNOT BE LIMITED UNDER APPLICABLE LAW." Excludes third-party-caused downtime, AI output errors, and loss of Customer Data except for gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**Governing Law and Dispute Resolution.** "governed by and construed under the laws of the State of Delaware... the state and federal courts located in Delaware will have exclusive jurisdiction... Any dispute with us must be brought on an individual basis and not as a plaintiff or class member in any purported class, consolidated, or representative proceeding. You and Lovable each waive any right to a jury trial." (No arbitration clause.)

**Survival.** IP, data rights, disclaimers, liability limits, indemnification, governing law survive.

**Copyrights and Illegal Content.** DMCA policy at lovable.dev/dmca; abuse reporting at lovable.dev/abuse; EU DSA contact dsa-contact@lovable.dev.

**Miscellaneous.** Publicity Rights (business entities' Marks license, revocable). Feedback: "we may use, copy, modify, distribute, publish, or otherwise exploit that Feedback for any purpose... without restriction or compensation to you." Entire Agreement (with Additional Terms). Assignment: "You may not assign... without our prior written consent. We may assign... without restriction." Waiver; Severability; Export Controls and Sanctions; Notices; Contact (support@lovable.dev).
